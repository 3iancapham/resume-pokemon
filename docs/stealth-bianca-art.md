## Prompt

Create one standalone battle trainer sprite: STEALTH BIANCA, the alter ego of the woman in the TOP LEFT of the reference atlas. Use that woman's exact recognizable face, long side-parted dark hair with chestnut ends, almond eyes, warm skin, and green jade pendant. Confident playful knowing smile. She wears a charcoal-purple open hooded jacket with hood up but long hair framing her clearly visible face, a white V-neck top, fitted dark trousers, white sneakers, jade pendant visible. One hand on hip, other hand holding a closed small dark laptop. No sunglasses, no mask. Match the reference's manga pixel art, dark pixel outlines, three-tone shading, full body and identical trainer proportions, crisp pixel clusters. SINGLE CHARACTER centered with generous empty margins, no text, no other characters. Solid pure magenta #ff00ff background for chroma key. The reference atlas is a style and identity reference; do not recreate its grid.
